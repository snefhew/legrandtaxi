Frequently asked questions

Booking, payment and Schiphol

How do I book a ride with Le Grand Services?

The quickest way is the form at the top of the page. Enter your pickup address and destination and you see your fixed price right away. Travelling to Schiphol? Add your flight number too. Prefer to talk it through, or need a ride at short notice? Call or message Michell on 06 85 63 90 77.

Do you run a meter, or is the price fixed?

We never run a meter. You agree a fixed price upfront and it stays put, even when traffic is heavy or your flight is delayed. So you know exactly what a taxi to Schiphol or to your own address costs before you get in.

What does a taxi to or from Schiphol cost?

For Schiphol we use fixed transfer rates. From Amsterdam the fare is 39 euro, from Almere 65 euro, from Utrecht 69 euro and from Rotterdam 99 euro. For any other address the form at the top of the page calculates your fixed price automatically.

How does Le Grand Services know my flight is delayed?

Give us your flight number when you book. We link it to the live flight status and track your flight in real time. If you land later than planned, the pickup time shifts along automatically. That waiting on a delay costs you nothing extra.

Where do I meet my driver at Schiphol?

You do not have to search. After you collect your baggage, Michell calls or messages you, usually within 15 minutes of landing. You agree a set spot in the arrivals hall, so you walk straight through to the car.

How can I pay?

Your choice. Upfront by iDEAL, in the car by debit or credit card, in cash, or on invoice for business travel. The fixed price stays the same either way.

Can I cancel my ride for free?

Yes. Up to 6 hours before the agreed pickup time you cancel at no charge. A quick call or message is enough. So you stay flexible if your plans change.

Do you also drive beyond Amsterdam and Schiphol?

Absolutely. We drive throughout the Netherlands, from any address to any address. Amsterdam, Haarlem, Hoofddorp, Amstelveen and the Schiphol routes are our best known trips, but an address further afield is just as easy to book. The form works out your fixed price for each route.

Do you offer business travel and invoicing?

Yes. For companies we drive to client meetings, the airport or regular commutes, smartly and on time. On request we invoice in your company name, so your travel costs add up neatly in the books.

How far in advance should I book?

Book well ahead where you can, so your taxi is ready at the agreed time for certain. For a ride at short notice, just call. We look straight away at what is possible.
